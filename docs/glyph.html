How to win

🟢 you · 🔵 AI. Walk into crates to push them — a whole row shifts at once if the cell beyond is empty (locked crates & pawns block).
Push a crate onto a goal to claim it. Your claims are locked to the rival — but you can still shove your own claimed crate off (it releases that goal), handy for clearing a path.
Crates sit off to the side of their goal — push one way, walk around to the next face, push again (an L). Don't shove a crate somewhere it can't come back from.
Block: race the rival to a crate, or shove a loose crate onto a goal to deny it.
Pass if you're stuck — if both pass in a row the game ends and the most claims wins.
First move is a coin-flip each game (it's a small edge), so it evens out.
★ boards are expert — long L-routes (push out, round the corner, push in); the AI thinks a little longer.
